About Emarat

Rooted in the UAE.
Built for tomorrow.

From the infrastructure behind industry to the stations at the heart of our communities, Emarat brings energy closer to the people and businesses who depend on it.

Our connected businesses span storage, commercial fuels, aviation, lubricants and everyday retail services. Together, they support a nation in motion.
